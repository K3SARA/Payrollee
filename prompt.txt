Localization & Statutory Compliance (Sri Lanka 2026):
The payroll engine must strictly implement Sri Lankan statutory deductions and tax rules:

EPF & ETF Calculations:

Employee EPF: Deduct exactly 8% from the employee's gross earnings.

Employer EPF: Calculate 12% of the employee's gross earnings as an employer cost (do not deduct from the employee).

Employer ETF: Calculate 3% of the employee's gross earnings as a separate employer cost (do not deduct from the employee).

APIT (PAYE) Tax Engine:

Calculate the employee's taxable income by taking the gross salary and deducting the employee's 8% EPF contribution.

Apply the statutory monthly tax-free personal relief of LKR 150,000.

For any remaining taxable income above LKR 150,000, apply these progressive monthly tax bands automatically:

First LKR 83,333.33 at 6%

Next LKR 41,666.67 at 18%

Next LKR 41,666.67 at 24%

Next LKR 41,666.67 at 30%

Any remaining balance at 36%

UI/Reporting Additions:

The generated payslip must clearly separate "Employee Deductions" (8% EPF + APIT) from "Employer Contributions" (12% EPF + 3% ETF).

Include a payroll summary report that shows the Total Cost to Company (Gross Salary + 15% Employer Contributions) for a given pay period.

Restrictions: Cannot access the global employee directory, payroll execution engine, or company financial reports. Attempts to access admin routes must automatically redirect to an "Unauthorized" page.
